## 2024-05-24 - [Title] Fix XSS in Plan Generators
 **Vulnerability:** The survey plan SVGs dynamically generated in `src/components/deedplan/DeedPlanGenerator.tsx` and `src/components/mutationplan/MutationPlanGenerator.tsx` were being directly rendered via `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` without proper sanitization. Even if data interpolation is partially escaped upstream, this poses a stored XSS vector.
 **Learning:** In client-rendered applications, all dynamically generated rich text or structured markups (like HTML/SVG) injected into the DOM via `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` should pass through defense-in-depth sanitization, specifically leveraging `DOMPurify` (or in this case, the `sanitizeHtml` wrapper from `@/lib/security/sanitize` customized for our SVG vocabulary).
 **Prevention:** Always use the local `sanitizeHtml` wrapper on variables before passing them into `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` when the contents contain any form of dynamic generation or user input. Ensure any new instances of `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` follow this convention.
