## 2024-09-28 - XSS Risk in Dynamically Generated SVGs Rendered via dangerouslySetInnerHTML
**Vulnerability:** Deed Plan and Mutation Plan SVG outputs were rendered using `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` or `document.write()` without sanitization in the presentation layer. Although `escapeXml` was used during SVG generation, defense-in-depth requires sanitizing the final output before rendering to prevent XSS.
**Learning:** Even if data is escaped during generation, the point of rendering (where `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` is used) remains a vulnerable sink. DOMPurify provides a robust defense and must be applied at this boundary, ensuring that only a safe, inert vocabulary of SVG elements is permitted. The project's custom DOMPurify wrapper handles this by allowing specific inert SVG tags and attributes while stripping vectors like scripts or external references.
**Prevention:** Always wrap dynamically generated or user-influenced HTML/SVG content with `sanitizeHtml` from `@/lib/security/sanitize` immediately before passing it to sinks like `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` or `document.write()`.
