Working goal established by the owner on 20 October 2026: make METARDU a

## Acceptance standard

A paying surveyor must be able to start with declared project control and
reference systems, collect and recover original observations, compute through
the established engines, review measured QA against adopted criteria, and
produce revision-bound deliverables that match the retained inputs. A failed,
unconfirmed or draft operation must remain visibly distinct from successful
delivery, field verification and professional approval.

Preserve the existing checkout, customer data, production/supporting services,
applied migration hashes and the localhost CAD training drawing. Follow
`AGENTS.md` and `DOCKER_RUNTIME_WORKFLOW.md`; use the existing source preview
and one reviewed production promotion. Do not create duplicate application
containers, bypass a rejected clone, prune broadly or manufacture field proof.
