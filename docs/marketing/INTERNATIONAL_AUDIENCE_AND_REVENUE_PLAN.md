# METARDU international audience and revenue campaign

Prepared 24 October 2026 (Africa/Nairobi). Owner correction: international clients are part of the intended audience now; Kenya is one market, not a geographic limit.

## Campaign objective

Win a small base of recurring paying survey professionals to help fund reliable hosting. Research and build conversations in the UK, Australia, South Africa and UAE, while retaining Kenya. Start with organic distribution and individual, permission-based workflow reviews. No market is assumed easier, richer, cheaper to acquire or ready to buy without evidence.

## Ideal customer

An owner, principal, survey manager or technical director at a small land, topographic or engineering-survey practice. Proposed qualification range: 2–20 staff or an active solo practitioner; it is an experiment filter, not measured headcount for the firms listed below.

Useful buyer signals:
- Recurring paid survey work and someone with authority over software purchasing.
- Repeated work checking field records/calculations and preparing CAD/data handovers for professional review.
- Willingness to inspect a synthetic or permission-cleared sample and compare with a known result.
- A concrete declared grid, units, axes, height reference, instrument/file format and expected deliverable.
- A workflow the demonstrated release can actually support.

Technical users may be land surveyors, engineering surveyors, survey technicians or CAD staff; the buyer is often the principal, owner or survey manager. Distinguish these roles.

Do not broadly target everyone called a “surveyor”: quantity surveyors, valuation-only practices, property brokers and home-inspection-only businesses may have little need for this product. Large enterprise procurement is a later hypothesis, not the first hosting-revenue dependency.

Example first milestone: **10 Pro accounts at USD 19 = USD 190 gross monthly recurring revenue**. A mixed example of 10 Pro plus 3 Team subscriptions is USD 295 gross monthly. These are arithmetic scenarios, not sales forecasts or a promise that hosting is covered.

If planning a 10% reserve (assumption, not measured fees/tax), USD 190 leaves USD 171 in the model. Illustrative operating-cost coverage:

| Assumed monthly bill | USD Pro-only accounts | USD Team-only subscriptions |
|---|---:|---:|
| USD 50 | 3 | 2 |
| USD 100 | 6 | 4 |
| USD 200 | 12 | 7 |

Formula: ceiling(cost / (price × 0.90)). Do not combine different currencies without a dated actual settlement/FX basis. Record the real bill for app/worker/database/storage/backups/email/monitoring/domain/support, provider deductions, tax, refunds and retention before treating any total as available hosting money. Monthly recurring revenue means active paying monthly subscriptions, not free accounts or one-off receipts.

## Fourteen-day international test

1. Days 1–2: make a list of 5 relevant firms per country (20 international firms) and 5 Kenyan firms. This is a research target, not completed outreach. Qualify service, current website, probable buyer and a concrete workflow. Validate the sample, payment/support path and expected operating bill.
2. Days 3–6: publish the master video/image organically when the owner is ready. Start individual conversations with existing contacts or people who permit a short product discussion. Aim for an initial 8 useful conversations across markets; that is a test target, not a promised response count.
3. Days 7–10: run about 4 guided sample walkthroughs based on actual interest. Ask them to inspect a calculation and output, name the present software/handoff, state local reference/file needs and identify the buyer.
4. Days 11–14: prioritize the 1–2 markets with useful product engagement and the fewest unresolved job-specific blockers. Offer a relevant existing subscription when workflow, payment and support fit are confirmed.

Track by country: firms researched, consented conversations, booked/completed walkthroughs, repeated use, reasons declined, paid subscriptions, support time and renewal. Do not use likes or broad reach as a revenue proxy. If nobody uses the workflow after the demo, fix that before buying reach.
